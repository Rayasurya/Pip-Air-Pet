# What the air was like before your marks

**SIMULATED DATA.** A made-up person, for testing this report.

Based on 71 marks. Each one is compared with the same time of day on the other days of the same month.

## Patterns worth testing

- **hours of stale air (CO2 above 1,400 ppm)** tends to be higher before your marks (0.2 h on average). It was higher than usual before 21 of 71 marks (30%), against 17% of ordinary days. Chance of a difference this big with no real link: 0.002.

A pattern is not proof. To test one, change it on purpose for two weeks (for example, keep CO2 under 1,000 ppm by airing the room) and see if marks drop.

## Weak hints (keep watching)

- **average room temperature** tends to be lower before your marks (0.1 C on average). It was lower than usual before 39 of 71 marks (55%), against 49% of ordinary days. Chance of a difference this big with no real link: 0.036.
- **average humidity** tends to be higher before your marks (0.3 % on average). It was higher than usual before 43 of 71 marks (61%), against 49% of ordinary days. Chance of a difference this big with no real link: 0.025.

These could be chance. Several things were checked, so one weak hint is expected now and then.

Pip only measures the room's air, light and (from the internet) outdoor air pressure. Sleep, food, stress and hormones also matter and are not seen here. Not medical advice.
