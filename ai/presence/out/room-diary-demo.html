What this page can and can't say

  • Someone here is the tested rule: CO2 stayed at least 80 ppm above the room's empty level for 5 minutes. The empty level is the room's own low point over the last 7 days.
  • Likely asleep and air clearing are reading aids on top of that rule. Their settings were tuned on the same days they are shown on, so they are not a tested result.
  • How sure is not a probability. Sure means any limit from 60 to 110 ppm gives the same answer for that block.
  • Pip was off means no readings. Nothing is guessed for that time.
  • Pip can't tell who is in the room, or how many people. It reads breathing, not faces.
  • This room is simulated. The thin dark line under each day is the simulator's truth: when someone was really in. A real room has no such line.
  • The simulated sensor is noisier than the real one, so the air never looks steady and likely asleep never shows here. Those nights read as someone here.
  • This is a demo or a saved copy, so Right and Wrong? work but are forgotten when you close the page.
